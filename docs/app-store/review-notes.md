# Notes for App Review

Paste into App Store Connect → your version → App Review Information.

## Sign-in information
Create this account yourself before submitting. Sign up in the app, finish onboarding with any companion, and send one message so the reviewer sees a real conversation.

- Username: `appreview@<your-domain>` (any inbox you control)
- Password: a strong password, also typed into App Store Connect

If email confirmation is on in Supabase, confirm this account first. Don't change its password while the app is in review.

## Notes (paste)
```
Thank you for reviewing this app.

WHAT IT IS
A Christian prayer and accountability companion. The user picks one of four illustrated characters (a mustard seed, a donkey, a sparrow, a lamb) and chats with it. Replies are generated by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude). The companion prays with the user, finds Bible passages, and checks in each morning and evening on a small commitment the user chose.

HOW TO TEST
1. Sign in with the demo account above (or create a new account with any email).
2. You'll land in the chat. Try: "I'm worried about a job interview tomorrow, can you pray for me?" or "What does the Bible say about worry?"
3. Settings (gear icon, top right) lets you switch companions, change Bible translation and check-in times, and turn reminders on or off.
4. Push notifications: reminders are sent at the user's chosen morning and evening times.

SAFETY AND ACCURACY
- Scripture is never generated from the model's memory. Verses are looked up from a database of public-domain translations (BSB, KJV, WEB, Douay-Rheims) and quoted exactly, with references.
- If a user mentions self-harm or crisis, the companion drops its playful personality and points to 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) and 911. A notice under the chat box states the companion is an AI, not a pastor or counselor, and gives 988.
- The companion never claims to be God or Jesus and doesn't give medical, legal or financial advice.
- Users can report any AI reply: long-press the reply, then tap "Report this reply".

PRIVACY
- Chats are private to the user and never shown to other users.
- No ads, no tracking, no data sales.
- Account deletion: Settings → Delete my account (permanent, removes all data on our servers).
```
