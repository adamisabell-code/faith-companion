| 1 | Support email is `support@example.com` | `apps/companion/src/lib/config.ts` | It shows in the app and on the support page. Apple rejects placeholder content, and users would write to a dead address. |

## One App Review risk to fix before submitting

**The Plus screen shows prices and plans that cannot be bought, plus two features labelled "COMING SOON".** Settings links to it in every build. Apple's rules say purchases shown in an app must be complete and working, and reviewers often reject unfinished or "coming soon" content (guidelines 2.1 and 3.1.1). The review notes do not mention the screen, so a reviewer would find it cold.

Two ways to handle it:

1. **Recommended:** hide the Plus button on the iPhone build until payments are live, and keep the "I'd want this" demand test on the web app. It is a few lines in `src/app/settings.tsx`. Say the word and I will make the change on a branch.
2. Keep it, and add a paragraph to the review notes explaining that it is a survey and takes no payment. This is the riskier choice.

## Things that are right and should stay

- Scripture comes only from the database, never from the model's memory.
- Crisis handling overrides every personality, and nobody is metered within a day of a safety event.
- The AI consent screen names the providers before any data is sent.
- Analytics are first-party and never contain what a user typed.
- Prayer and Scripture are never paywalled.
