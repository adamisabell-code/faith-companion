# Amen Buddy: 30-day content calendar

November 13, 2026. For TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. No one appears on camera.

## The format

Every video is the same three beats, 12 to 20 seconds long:

1. **Hook (0 to 3 s):** the line from the table as on-screen text over the buddy on its home screen.
2. **Moment (3 to 12 s):** a screen recording of the real app doing the thing the hook promised.
3. **Close (last 3 s):** the buddy and the words "Amen Buddy. Free to start."

This copies what worked for the closest comparable apps: one script, many hooks, posted until one repeats. [Prayer Lock](https://rosetta.to/u/starterstory/i-built-a-20k-month-mobile-app-here-s-my-entire-playbook) reached about $1K a month on free posts before spending on ads, and [Pray Screen](https://api.superwall.me/blog/he-built-a-simple-usd120k-yr-app-then-sold-it-for-multiple-6-figures) ran 10 to 12 accounts testing hooks.

## Rules

- **Only show what the app really does.** Record the real app. No mock-ups of features that are "coming soon".
- **First-person claims must be true.** Hooks marked ★ say something happened to you. Post them only once it has happened to you or to a tester who agreed. Until then use the reworded version in brackets.
- **Never show a real person's prayer request, commitment or name.** Use a demo account with made-up entries.
- **Scripture on screen comes from the app**, in a public-domain translation, with its reference, exactly as the app shows it.
- **No promises about God.** The app helps people show up. It does not guarantee answers.

## Reading the results

- Each Sunday, sort the week's videos by views after 24 hours. A hook that beats your average by three times or more gets five new versions the next week.
- Day 1 and day 14 are built for comments. If "which buddy are you" draws replies, make one video per buddy answering them.
- Add the web app's address to each profile once it is deployed. Installs from video cannot be measured until then (`docs/strategy.md`, section 6).
- Do not pay to promote anything until the dashboard shows what a user costs (`supabase/dashboard.sql`, query 8).
